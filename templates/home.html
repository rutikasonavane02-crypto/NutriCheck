NutriCheck

Nutrition Deficiency Analysis

A Community Engagement Project that analyzes nutrition-related survey data and highlights possible nutrition-risk areas related to Iron, Vitamin B12, Vitamin D and Calcium.

🚀 Get Started – Analyze My Nutrition

What NutriCheck Analyzes

🩸

Iron

Analyzes dietary patterns associated with possible iron-risk areas.

🧠

Vitamin B12

Analyzes dietary patterns associated with possible Vitamin B12-risk areas.

☀️

Vitamin D

Considers daylight exposure and related lifestyle patterns.

🦴

Calcium

Considers dairy and other dietary patterns related to calcium intake.

How NutriCheck Works

1

Answer

Complete the nutrition questionnaire.

2

Store

Your responses are stored for project analysis.

3

Analyze

The system analyzes selected nutrition responses.

4

Results

View possible nutrition-risk areas.

Important: NutriCheck is a student educational project. Results indicate possible nutrition-risk areas based on survey responses and are not a medical diagnosis.
