Nutrition Guide

Learn about important nutrients and common food sources.

🩸 Iron

Iron helps the body make healthy red blood cells.

Food Sources

  • Green leafy vegetables
  • Lentils and beans
  • Chickpeas
  • Fortified cereals
  • Eggs and meat
  • Nuts and seeds

🧠 Vitamin B12

Vitamin B12 supports normal blood and nerve function.

Food Sources

  • Milk and dairy products
  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Meat and poultry
  • Fortified foods

☀️ Vitamin D

Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium and supports bone health.

Sources

  • Appropriate sunlight exposure
  • Fortified milk and foods
  • Egg yolks
  • Fatty fish
  • Some mushrooms

🦴 Calcium

Calcium is important for bones, teeth and normal muscle function.

Food Sources

  • Milk and yoghurt
  • Cheese
  • Ragi
  • Sesame seeds
  • Green leafy vegetables
  • Calcium-fortified foods

🥗 General Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.
  • Include protein-rich foods such as pulses and beans.
  • Choose a variety of foods from different food groups.
  • Drink water regularly according to your individual needs.
  • Limit excessive intake of highly processed foods.
  • Seek professional advice for individual nutrition concerns.
