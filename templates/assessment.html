Community Nutrition Assessment

Welcome! This assessment helps analyze dietary patterns and possible nutrition-risk areas for educational purposes.

Before you start

  • Answer all 20 questions.
  • Choose the option that best describes you.
  • The assessment takes approximately 3–5 minutes.
  • Your responses are stored for the NutriCheck community survey project.
  • The results are educational indicators and do not diagnose nutritional deficiencies.
