📊 What does this result mean?

NutriCheck analyzes selected dietary and lifestyle responses to identify areas where there may be a possible nutrition risk.

  • A Lower possible risk means your survey responses did not trigger the project's defined risk indicators for that nutrient.
  • A Moderate possible risk means some responses matched the project's nutrition-risk indicators.
  • A Higher possible risk means more of the project's defined indicators were present in your responses.

These categories are part of the NutriCheck Data Science project and should not be interpreted as a medical diagnosis.

🥗 General Nutrition Guidance

A balanced diet can include a variety of nutrient-rich foods. Some general examples are:

🩸 Iron

Include a variety of iron-containing foods such as pulses, beans, leafy vegetables, fortified foods, eggs, fish, or meat according to your dietary preferences.

🧠 Vitamin B12

B12 can be obtained from foods such as eggs, dairy products, fish, meat, and fortified foods, depending on your diet.

☀️ Vitamin D

Vitamin D can come from appropriate sunlight exposure, foods containing vitamin D, and fortified foods.

🦴 Calcium

Calcium-containing foods include dairy products and suitable fortified alternatives, as well as some leafy vegetables and other calcium-containing foods.
